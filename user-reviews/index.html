Sharing Your Psychic Reading Experience

Your voice matters! By sharing your experiences, you contribute to a valuable community resource that helps countless others navigate their psychic journey. Whether your experience was profoundly insightful or left you with questions, your feedback is crucial. Here’s how you can share your review and what to consider:

  • Be Specific: The more details you provide about your reading, the more helpful your review will be. Mention the psychic’s name, the type of reading you received, and any specific insights or predictions that stood out.
  • Focus on the Experience: Describe the overall flow of the reading, the psychic’s communication style, their empathy, and how well they connected with your situation.
  • Be Respectful: Even if your experience wasn’t what you hoped for, please maintain a respectful tone. Constructive criticism is valuable; personal attacks are not.

How to read a review critically

Reviews are stories told by individuals, often shortly after an emotional experience. They can be sincere and still incomplete. A reviewer who feels moved may rate highly without checking whether the details were accurate, and a reviewer disappointed by an unwelcome prediction may rate low for the same reason. Read for facts such as price, timing, and support rather than only for feelings.

Signs a review may not be reliable

  • Very short praise with no detail about the session
  • Several reviews with near-identical wording
  • A sudden burst of five-star ratings in a short period
  • Reviews that read like advertising copy or repeat the provider’s slogans
  • Reviewers who mention being offered an incentive or free time in exchange

Incentivised reviews are not forbidden everywhere, but they should be disclosed, and a reader should discount them accordingly.

Why negative reviews are useful

Complaints often reveal what a service does when things go wrong. Look for patterns, for example repeated mentions of unexpected charges, difficulty getting a refund, or pressure to extend a session. A single angry review matters less than the same complaint appearing many times, and a provider that responds politely and fixes problems is a better sign than one that never replies.

Writing a useful review yourself

If you decide to leave feedback, include the date, what you paid, how long the session lasted, what you asked, and which statements you could later check. Say what did not match as well as what did. Avoid sharing personal details about yourself or others, and be fair rather than punitive.

Reviews and grief

People who are grieving may be especially affected by a reading, and their reviews may reflect relief and gratitude. That is a real experience, though it differs from independent evidence of accuracy. When comparing reviews of mediumship, give extra weight to those describing checkable details.
